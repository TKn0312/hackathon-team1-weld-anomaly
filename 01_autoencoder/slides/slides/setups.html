The four classes, one typical real X-ray tile each: no defect (even grain), porosity (round dark blob from a trapped gas bubble), crack (thin dark line), lack of penetration (straight dark band along the weld). Dataset: RIAWELC. Before training we removed 2,514 duplicates (every original test image was also in training) and rebuilt the split by weld, so test welds are truly unseen. The autoencoder trains only on the 3,783 sound tiles. We compared four setups on the same split and seed. D, the smallest (convolutional, 64 px, code of 32 numbers, 0.7 M parameters), has the best validation AUC (0.990) and is best on every defect type. Why: 64 px averages away the X-ray grain, a bigger code lets defects through, and the dense model ignores shapes. All autoencoders beat the contrast-only baseline, most on cracks (0.985 vs 0.926). We expected 128 px to win; the comparison showed 64 px is better. Full table in the README.
