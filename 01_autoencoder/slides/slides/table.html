This is the full comparison table the homework asks for. Every setup used the same split, seed and training recipe, and trained on sound tiles only. Errors are the mean reconstruction error of sound tiles: lower means it rebuilds sound welds better. ROC AUC says how well the error separates defects from sound tiles. Precision and recall are on the test set at the 95% threshold. Setup D wins on every count that matters: best validation AUC, best recall, lowest error, and the fewest parameters (0.7 M, 23 times fewer than the dense model). Test scores are all very high because the 4 test welds are easier; the winner was chosen on validation only, and about 0.99 is the honest claim. Precision looks almost perfect partly because the test set has 2.5 times more defect tiles than sound ones.
