Inside the network, as it reads your weld

Team 1's autoencoder, running live in this page. Your weld enters on the left, is squeezed into 32 numbers, and is redrawn on the right. Every plane is a real feature map from that pass. Click a layer to see all of its channels; drag the 32 bars to change what the decoder receives.

What is real here: every plane, bar and number is computed by the published model in your browser (two ONNX files exported from its weights and checked against the deployed model and PyTorch). Planes show each layer's five most active channels for this weld, coloured on that layer's own scale; small bars are per-channel mean activations. The dotted lines only show the order of operations. They are not weights, and they do not show which neurons connect.
